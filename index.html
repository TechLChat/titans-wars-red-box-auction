Boxes 31 & 32 — high & low power queues

Box 31 and Box 32 are extra Red Boxes.
You do not keep them. They move to a new player each cycle.
You must pay diamonds to get the extra box.

Find your name in the list below.
Do you have enough diamonds? Do you want the extra box?
Yes. Buy the box. Then change your status to Completed /accepted.
No. Change your status to Declined. The box goes to the next player.

It is okay to say no. You can get the box in the next cycle.

You can only change your status when your name is marked NEXT.
Before that, your box is locked. Please wait your turn.
